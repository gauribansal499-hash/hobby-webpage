The Art of Paper Planes

Paper planes are a fun and creative way to explore the principles of aerodynamics.

Why Paper Planes?

It requires no expensive material just a single sheet of paper.

Materials Needed

To make a paper plane, you will need:

  • A sheet of paper
  • Scissors (optional)

Steps-by-Step Folding Instructions

  1. Start with a rectangular sheet of paper.
  2. Fold the paper in half lengthwise and then unfold it to create a crease.
  3. Fold the top corners down to the center crease to form a triangle.
  4. Fold the triangle down to meet the bottom edge of the paper.
  5. Fold the top corners down again to meet the center crease.
  6. Fold the paper in half along the original center crease.
  7. Fold the wings down on each side to create the plane's wings.
  8. Adjust the wings and tail for balance and stability.
